    The Heart of Home Economics

    Some people keep a house. Nitasha makes a home.

    “Creating a product is never just about putting ingredients together. It is about the thought behind every choice.”

    For Nitasha Arora, a home has never been just four walls. It is a feeling — built from the small things chosen with care, every single day. Home Economics is that belief, bottled.

    Driven by a curious mind and a genuine love for homegrown essentials, she brings together the wisdom of nature and the possibilities of science — experimenting, refining and re-pouring until every formula is something she would happily welcome into her own home. No shortcuts, no noise: just honest, small-batch essentials, made with the same care she has always given her own family.

    Curious MindAlways exploring better, more thoughtful solutions for everyday living.
    Care & ScienceAncient botanical wisdom meets modern formulation.
    Genuine LoveOnly what she would use in her own home.
    Read Nitasha’s note to our community

    Home Economics was born from something very simple yet deeply meaningful to me — a genuine love for homegrown essentials and a curious mind that has always believed there is more to discover, more to create, and more to bring into everyday life.

    What began with a passion slowly grew into a vision: to create products that are not only effective, but also thoughtful, purposeful, and made with an understanding of what people truly need.

    At Home Economics, we believe that the best solutions often come from bringing together the wisdom of nature and the possibilities of science. Every product is thoughtfully developed with carefully selected ingredients, each chosen for its unique properties and purpose.

    We have poured our time, passion, ideas, and countless moments of experimentation into every product, and it is incredibly fulfilling to finally share them with you. I hope that when you use Home Economics, you experience not just a product, but the thought, care, and passion that went into creating it.

    Thank you for welcoming Home Economics into your home and becoming a part of our journey.

    With warmth and gratitude,

    Nitasha Arora
