Core Design Principle
Remove unnecessary friction digitally — but preserve human reassurance at high-stakes moments.

7 Key Stages of the Redesigned Journey
01
AWARENESS
📣
“This is relevant to me.”
Life-stage based messaging.

Trigger questions:
• What happens when you change jobs?
• What happens when the bill exceeds your employer coverage?
Outcome: I see the protection gap
02
DISCOVER
📝
“Understand my need.”
8–10 smart, adaptive questions.

Ask only what matters and explain why.
Outcome: I understand my gaps
03
RECOMMEND
🎯
“I understand why.”
Introduce a personalised Protection Map showing:

• Current situation
• Protection gaps
• Why this plan fits
Outcome: I trust the recommendation
04
COMPARE & DECIDE
⚖️
“I know what I'm buying.”
Compare maximum three plans.

Show price, coverage, exclusions and real claim examples.
Outcome: I can choose confidently
05
PURCHASE
💳
“No surprises.”
Transparent exclusions acknowledged before payment.

Digital payment + simplified KYC + save/resume.
Outcome: I buy with confidence
06
ONBOARD
🧭
“I know what to do next.”
Instant activation.

Protection dashboard + documents + welcome walkthrough.
Outcome: I feel prepared
07
SERVICE & CLAIMS
🎧
“Someone has my back.”
AI handles simple queries.

Human support handles important moments, with a named claim owner.
Outcome: My problem is resolved

Awareness

Create relevance before selling insurance. Move from generic advertising to life-stage situations that make the protection gap visible.

Customer action Recognises a personal protection gap.
Technology Segment-led landing page + contextual messaging.
Human role Not required yet; customer controls the journey.

Failure-Proofing at This Stage

Primary risk Customer does not see insurance as relevant.
Design response Use concrete life situations rather than generic insurance advertising.
Success signal Customer continues into the needs-assessment journey.
Service Blueprint — Purchase, Onboarding & Claims Assistance

Three Layers of Service Delivery

CUSTOMER FACING

Purchase

View protection map → compare ≤3 plans → review coverage & exclusions → pay → complete KYC

Onboarding

Policy activation → access dashboard → understand benefits → receive reminders

Claims

Raise claim → upload documents → track status → speak to claim owner

MIDDLE LAYER

Service Enablers

AI needs assessment, pricing validation, advisor-on-demand and KYC assistance.

Service Enablers

Welcome support, incomplete-KYC alerts, CRM updates and personalised reminders.

Service Enablers

AI triage, named claim owner, proactive updates and escalation.

BACK OFFICE

Behind the Scenes

Behind the Scenes

Policy issuance → document generation → customer profile update → secure document vault

Behind the Scenes

Purchase

No hidden exclusions; real-time eligibility; save & resume.

Onboarding

KYC alerts; assisted completion; policy dashboard.

Claims

Named owner; clear SLA; proactive status updates.
